## 63. The 8 s scored window was a PROPOSAL, not a requirement

The 8 s held-out evaluation window was **Main's proposal**, **not** a user requirement and **not
frozen**. What *is* mandatory is the **full five-recording DREGON cohort** (addendum 8 §42 item 2).

Therefore, if 8 s does **not** fit all five recordings under the support guards:

- **Propose one common, shorter scored window length applied to ALL FIVE** — e.g. the same 4 s
  scored cruise window across every recording, with valid surrounding context.
- **Never** drop a recording, **never** repeat, **never** pad. Those were already prohibited; a
  short recording is a reason to change the *common* duration, not to lose a cluster.
- The scored length must be the **same across all five**. No per-recording durations, which would
  reintroduce per-window selection.
- **Window duration may be frozen on the basis of BASELINE COVERAGE ONLY, and before any
  candidate.** Never chosen or revised after seeing candidate results.

Known concrete risk to check: the **`spinning_nosource_room2` record is ~41 s** and may not leave
an 8 s disjoint held-out support once the guards (fit-support exclusion, filter/context margins)
are applied.

Then **propose the single frozen duration to Main with those numbers**. If 8 s fits all five,

## 66. `RENDER_TRANSFER_SPEC` — two agreed keys are MISSING

Verified by running your landed helper: it works and is correct. Measured composite power gain at

| f (Hz) | gain |
|---|---|
| 1000 | +0.009 dB |
| 7500 | -1.845 dB |
| 7900 | **-4.912 dB** |
| 7950 | -11.466 dB |

The -4.912 dB at 7900 Hz matches Main's independently measured ~4.9 dB decimator rolloff, so the
composition is right. `RENDER_TRANSFER_SPEC` is also correctly a **plain JSON-serializable dict**
(verified with `json.dumps`; every leaf is str/float/list) — keep it that way, because the
candidate export writes it verbatim into `training_provenance.render_transfer_spec` and
`stage2.save`'s `plain()` would raise on a dataclass or a numpy scalar. **Never** put a numpy
float in it.

Two keys agreed with the candidate owner are **absent**; current keys are only
`['components', 'antialias', 'decimator', 'normalization', 'approximation']`. Add:

- **`aa_application`** = `"single_pass_centered_fir"`. This records that the AA contributes
  `|H_aa|^2` and **not** `|H|^4`, so no export can ever be ambiguous about which transfer was
  multiplied in. It is the whole point of having replaced `filtfilt`.
- **`alias_floor_db`** — a **COMPUTED** worst-case number, not an assertion. Evaluate the designed
  response at the frequencies that fold into `0..8000` under 4:1 decimation and store the worst
  attenuation among them, alongside the `>= 100 dB` expectation. Both the candidate owner and I
  need to be able to **cite** this number rather than assert it; it is what justifies treating the
  transfer as purely multiplicative.

## 67. Two hard constraints on the transfer, agreed with the candidate owner

**a) `RENDER_TRANSFER_SPEC` keys are ADD-ONLY. Never rename an existing key.**
The candidate export copies the dict **verbatim**, so exports written before your next pass will
carry today's keys. Adding `aa_application` and `alias_floor_db` is free and they will appear in
candidate exports automatically. Renaming `components`, `antialias`, `decimator`,
`normalization` or `approximation` later would strand already-written exports with a key
`--check` no longer recognises. Additive only.

**b) The FLOOR must be multiplied by the transfer exactly ONCE, just like the lines.**
On the candidate side the coloured floor now goes through the shared kernel as a proper complex
process (real atom `w(t)*A_floor(t)`, `r_tau = 2*R_floor`), so floor and lines get the same
oversampling and the **same single** transfer multiplication.

Your legacy adapter may keep the floor on its own path — that is expected — **but it must also be
multiplied by the transfer exactly once.** A floor that misses the ~4.9 dB at 7900 Hz while the
lines carry it is the **subtlest possible way for the two families to disagree at the band
edge**, and it would surface as a bogus baseline-vs-candidate difference rather than as an
obvious bug. Assert the single application on both the line path and the floor path.

Mirror the candidate's discipline: evaluate the gain **once** into a cached buffer and multiply at
**exactly one site**, so double application is structurally impossible rather than merely
avoided.
