## 7. Primary tracker score reference — FROZEN

- The primary HPPNet score reference is the **SAME RAW TELEMETRY ARRAY** for the real arm and
  for the baseline/candidate synthetic arms. One array, both arms.
- Report it explicitly as **reference agreement**, not as proven ground truth.
- **Never switch the primary target between arms.** Never use a refined or posterior held-out
  trajectory as the reference for synthetic prediction.
  - Note the distinction: the exports carry `rps_key rps_refined` as *fit provenance*. That
    does not make the refined track the scoring reference. Scoring reference = raw telemetry.
- A candidate renderer additionally emits its **realized physical shaft RPS** for a
  **diagnostic-only** MAE. Diagnostic, never a gate, never the primary target.

## 8. Spectral score — FROZEN form

- Frozen form: **positive overlap-exposure weighted marginal `I/M + logM`**, normalized per
  unique duration.
- Call it a **COMPOSITE** score. Do **not** call it, name it, or document it as an exact joint
  NLL. No percentage NLL.
- **Preserve duplicate-window exposure invariance**: duplicating a window must not change the
  score. Make this an explicit regression test — it is the invariant most likely to break.
- Baseline implementation may use **generic predicted-`M` adapters**. Do **not** build a full
  dense STFT covariance; a dense Gaussian likelihood over redundant complete STFT arrays is
  singular unless handled on its true support, and it is out of scope here.

## 9. Statistical pairing — FROZEN

- DREGON: **whole-recording** pairing, 5 clustered units (the five no-source room2 recordings).
- Michael's: **conditional FLY124 blocks**. Conditional on that one recording — never an
  across-recording population-95 claim.
