## Gap 1 — §2.2 says nothing about WHICH samples enter each regime's MAE

`## 2.2 Michael's rig — fixed-recording gate on FLY124` defines
`E_rig = (E_standby + E_ramp + E_cruise)/3` and the `<= 1.05` gate, but never says what interval
each regime MAE is computed over. That is an integrity hole: the Michael **ramp can be shorter
than an 8 s context window**, so an 8 s average that is mostly standby or cruise could be
reported as "ramp MAE". Addendum 4 §26 calls this the single easiest way to fake this gate.

Add a `::: {.callout-warning}` (or a short subsection) inside §2.2, after the aggregate-ratio
material, stating:

- Each regime MAE is computed **only** on the outputs whose times lie inside that regime's
  **pre-registered raw-telemetry support**. Standby and cruise are likewise scored on their
  **declared support**.
- Surrounding waveform is **context only**. The whole-window context plus the complete
  STFT/filter guard may legitimately extend beyond the scored target interval — that is
  expected, and it is **not** licence to score the extra material.
- **Never** label a mostly-standby or mostly-cruise 8 s average as "ramp MAE".
- **Regime definitions are fixed and selected BEFORE any candidate.** Regime boundaries are
  never inferred from a candidate, and never from a refined or posterior track (consistent with
  the refined-track-is-diagnostic-only rule already in §2.4).
- The masks and the scored times are retained as **provenance** in the saved record, so a reader
  can check which samples entered which regime's MAE.

## Gap 2 — §2.3's LTAS gate is one line, and the initial gate forms are missing

`## 2.3 The separate spectral and LTAS gates` fully specifies the composite risk but reduces the
LTAS gate to "a **baseline-calibrated LTAS** gate", and the document never records the frozen
initial gate forms or the level-normalization rule. Expand that bullet (and add the gate forms
after it) with, from addendum 3 §17–§20:

**LTAS level convention (§17, §18).**

- The gate is on **ABSOLUTE-level LTAS error**. **No arm-specific peak or band normalization.**
  A relative-shape LTAS may be reported as a **secondary diagnostic** only.
- If any unit-mean periodogram scaling is used, the scale is derived **once from the real clip**
  and the **same** scale is applied to **all** models and arms. **Never** normalize a synthetic
  arm independently — per-arm normalization would let an arm launder a level error into an
  apparent shape match.
- Cross-reference the already-present note that the examined render path does not preserve
  fitted absolute levels through its RMS-normalising wrapper, and that this is a pending item of
  the frozen protocol with **no number published for it**. (That note already exists near the
  head of the document — point at it, do not restate it, and add no numbers.)

**Initial spectral gate — frozen form (§19).**

- **DREGON composite:** paired mean $\Delta$composite $< 0$ **and** the upper one-sided 95 %
  bound $< 0$.
- **DREGON LTAS:** point non-regression, plus a **baseline-variability-calibrated tolerance**
  for the uncertainty allowance.
- **Michael's:** composite/LTAS non-regression tolerances, **conditional on FLY124**, calibrated
  on baseline data **before** any candidate is evaluated.

**Null-variation calibration (§20).**

- The exact null-variation calibration rule is **frozen before candidates run**.
- **Tolerances are never tuned after seeing candidate results.** The instrument reads the
  calibration from the frozen manifest and **fails loudly if the manifest lacks it**, rather than
  defaulting to something permissive.
- The document already has a sentence to this effect further down (near the freeze-manifest
  section). Keep both consistent; a cross-reference is better than a duplicated paragraph.
