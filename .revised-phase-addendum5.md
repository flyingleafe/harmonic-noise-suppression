# Addendum 5 — exposure weighting is at the WRONG granularity (required fix)

Precedence: addendum 5 > addendum 4 > 3 > 2 > addendum > brief. Main reviewed `revised_eval.py`
again. This one is a correctness bug against the overlap contract you were already given, not a
new statistical model. Confirmed in your source.

## 27. The defect

`exposure_weights` (revised_eval.py ~802) builds `spans_by_rec` from **whole `Window` spans**
— clip-level `start_s`/`end_s`. So exposure counts how many **8 s evaluation clips** cover a
point. It does **not** count overlap or duplication of the **1.024 s STFT analysis windows**.

Consequences:

- Within one clip, all ~110 overlapping analysis frames receive weight 1.
- Duplicating a **frame** entry inside a `FrameScore` still **doubles** its contribution to the
  score. Only duplicating a whole clip is currently neutralized.

Related inconsistency to fix while you are there: the module docstring (~29-34) says "the frames
overlap nothing at ``HOP == N_FFT``", and ~1364-1366 says window-level exposure weighting rather
than frame counting "carries the evidence bookkeeping". Both statements are false under the
frozen observation convention of **NFFT 16384 with hop 1024** (addendum 2 §13): frames overlap
16-fold, ~110 frames per 8 s clip. Correct the prose along with the code.

## 28. Required behaviour

- Tie positive exposure to the **actual analysis-frame supports and cadence**, not to clip spans.
- **Split identical duplicates** keyed on `(recording, absolute frame center, n_fft/window)`
  wherever such entries are stored. Identical observed support must share one unit of exposure
  between its copies.
- Minimum acceptable implementation: an explicit **hop/window exposure factor** combined with
  **duplicate-analysis-frame weighting**, normalized consistently **per unique observed
  support**.
- Keep it **simple**. A fixed hop is a **decision-risk** setting; it must **not** be presented as
  exact information content. Do not build a new statistical model or a second metric.
- Calibrate the **fit temperature separately** from this exposure bookkeeping — they are
  different knobs and must not be conflated.

## 29. Required regression tests

- **Duplicate analysis frame** (not merely a duplicate whole clip): duplicating a single frame
  entry must leave the composite score unchanged. This is the test that would have caught the
  present bug, so it must be observable at the frame level.
- **Constant-risk hop-density sanity check**: evaluating the same material at a different hop
  density must leave the risk per unique second approximately constant, rather than scaling with
  the number of frames.

Keep both cheap and deterministic. No project-wide validation; no baseline job; no freeze until
Main authorizes. Record the exposure convention you implement in
`docs/revised-phase-baseline-proposals.md`.
