# Addendum 10 — SIMPLIFY: delete instead of hardening (supersedes parts of addendum 8)

Precedence: addendum 10 > 9 > 8 > 7 > 6 > 5 > 4 > 3 > 2 > addendum > brief.

Main's ruling: the simplicity requirement (addendum 2 §12) applies to **your** evaluator scope
too. Two addendum-8 items should be resolved by **removing code**, not by growing machinery.
This is a permitted **clean cutover of code introduced during this campaign**.

## 49. Delete the `wav_dir` / manual-audio candidate route

Manual-audio candidates are **not required by this goal**. The only arms this campaign scores
are:

- pinned **legacy baseline exports**, and
- **revised model exports** (`model_family='shared_shaft_ou'`).

So **delete the `wav_dir` route entirely** rather than expanding shape/provenance machinery to
make it safe. This replaces addendum 8 §42 item 5's "validate it" with "remove it" — the
truncation bug disappears with the route.

What must still hold on the **actual render paths**:

- enforce **exact audio and RPS length**, and
- enforce **8 channels** exactly.

Still no silent truncation and no implicit mic slicing — just enforced on the real paths rather
than on a hypothetical user-supplied WAV.

## 50. Delete the candidate seed override

Remove the `candidate.seeds` override outright (addendum 8 §42 item 7) rather than supporting it
and validating equality. Every arm uses the **frozen render seed list**. Deleting the extra knob
is the simpler and safer resolution.

## 51. No plugin framework

Do **not** build a generic plugin/adapter framework for arm types. Two concrete families,
dispatched on `model_family` (addendum 8 §41), is the whole requirement.

## 52. Scope of deletion

This licence covers **only code introduced during this campaign**. **Preserve unrelated user
work** — the inherited modifications to `clips.py`, `stage1_bayes.py`, `stage2.py`,
`_stage2_panels.py`, `_ab_render.py`, `_fit_params_panel.py` and
`docs/handoff-stochastic-fit.md` stay. Do not delete anything you did not add, and do not touch
candidate-owned or docs-owned files.

## 53. Hard guard on `diagnostics.identified`

The candidate fit CLI currently continues and saves even when `ShaftDynamics.identified == False`,
so an **unidentifiable frozen lam/sigma fit can become scoreable**.

The evaluator is the integrity boundary, so the binding guard is **yours**:

- Read `diagnostics.identified` from the candidate export.
- If it is `False`, **hard-fail the check** — refuse to score the arm, exit nonzero, and record
  the reason in `gates.json`. Do not score it and flag it; do not downweight it; do not treat it
  as a warning.
- If the field is **missing** on an export that declares `model_family='shared_shaft_ou'`, that is
  also a failure — an unidentifiable fit must not pass by omission.
- Keep it to one cheap check at export-read time (addendum 8 §41 dispatch), consistent with the
  no-machinery rule.

This is a scoring-integrity guard, not a modelling judgement: an unidentifiable dynamics fit has
no meaningful lam/sigma to compare, so a number derived from it must never enter a gate.
