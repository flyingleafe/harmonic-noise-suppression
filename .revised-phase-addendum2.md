## 13. Frozen spectral observation convention — use exactly these

- Existing 16 kHz convention. Default long **periodic Hann**, **NFFT 16384**, **hop 1024**
  (64 ms) for the spectrum.
- **Fixed band 30..7900 Hz**, fixed mics. **Never** lower the fit band to conceal filter loss
  — note this interacts with your AA work: the 7900 Hz passband edge must stay intact.
- Composite risk, frozen form: `L = sum_i a_i * (I_i / M_i + log M_i)`, positive weights `a_i`
  **normalized per unique audio duration**, fixed band and mics.
- **Duplicate identical observation windows must SPLIT exposure**, never double the score or
  the evidence. Regression-test this.
- Call it a marginal **composite** risk. It is proper for mean prediction despite correlation.
  It is **not** an exact joint NLL, not evidence, not a posterior. Cellwise Hessians and
  nominal frame counts cannot supply uncertainty.
- Uncertainty: DREGON whole-recording paired bootstrap; Michael's block variation
  **conditional on FLY124**.
- No dense Gaussian over a redundant full STFT pretending to a nonsingular covariance. Generic
  predicted-`M` adapters only.
