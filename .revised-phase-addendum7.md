Precedence: addendum 7 > 6 > 5 > 4 > 3 > 2 > addendum > brief. This is the concrete answer to

## 38. TWO real unit conversions — do not transfer numbers without them

**1. Amplitude convention.** `A = sqrt(2P)` makes `P` the tone's **mean square**.
`model.CombSpectrum.line_power()` is multiplied by a **unit-area density**, so *its* `P` is a
**band weight in periodogram*Hz**. These differ by a fixed factor. **Never transfer `profile_db`
numerically between a legacy export and a candidate export without applying the conversion.**
This is precisely the silent factor Main warned about; state it explicitly wherever levels cross
between the two families.

**2. Renderer floor units.** A periodogram in `data.periodogram` units reads `P_phys * sr/2`.
Consequently broadband noise shaped at 44.1 kHz reads `16000/44100` — **-4.4 dB** — lower after
decimation, while **tones are unaffected**. The candidate renderer pre-compensates by
`sqrt(sample_rate_native / sample_rate)`.

> **Diagnostic shortcut:** if your legacy-adapter-vs-renderer-mean-statistics validation shows a
> floor offset of about **-4.4 dB** with tones matching, that is this sample-rate effect — **not**
> a kernel error and not a fit error. Identify it as such rather than absorbing it into a fudge
> factor. Tones matching while the floor sits 4.4 dB low is the signature.

Both facts belong in the declared conversion recorded in
`docs/revised-phase-baseline-proposals.md`, together with your pure-tone and floor validation
numbers.

## 40. Measured kernel accuracy — calibrate your adapter tolerances against these

From the kernel owner's numpy-only check (`n_fft=64`, moving carrier + amplitude ramp,
`R = exp(-0.031*tau)`, 40k Monte-Carlo draws):

- vs explicit double sum: max relative error **6.109e-16**
- `max|Im P_Z| / max Re P_Z` = **5.384e-17** (P_Z real); min/max `P_Z` = 5.783e-04, non-negative,
  nothing clipped
- batched `(3,2,F)` broadcast vs looped: **0.000e+00**
- fractional-bin tone `f = 7.3/N` vs MC mean: max|MC - pred| **5.762e-06** (2 s.e. 1.329e-01)
- tone + diffusion: **2.270e-02** (2 s.e. 9.542e-02); chirp + amplitude ramp + diffusion:
  **1.156e-02** (2 s.e. 8.142e-02)
- `R == 1` vs folded `|Z|^2`: **5.031e-17**

Do not set adapter tolerances tighter than the Monte-Carlo standard errors above; the kernel
itself is exact to roundoff, so any larger discrepancy you see is in **your** adapter or in the
unit conversions of §38.
