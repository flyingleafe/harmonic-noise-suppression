## F6 — The Wiener increment-kernel reason is false; replace it with the cross-term argument (priority 2)

`docs/explainers/bench-fit-revised-phase-model.qmd`, ~1707–1714 (render contract item 3), and the
same claim restated in the §6.2 conditional/marginal callout (~1192–1195) and in
`fitting-four-moving-harmonic-combs.qmd` §2.3.

Current reason: with $\epsilon_{rk}(0)=0$ *"the residual process has a non-stationary mean and
cross-terms, $\exp(-D_r|\tau|)$ is no longer its increment kernel"*.

**The increment-kernel half is mathematically false.** A deterministic $\epsilon(0)$ cancels in the
difference: $\epsilon(t)-\epsilon(s)$ is unaffected by the initial constant, so
$E\exp\{i[\epsilon(t)-\epsilon(s)]\}=\exp(-D|t-s|)$ **regardless** of the initial condition.

**The conclusion still holds, for a different reason.** Independent uniform initial phases are
still required — they make each phasor **zero-mean**, which is what eliminates the
**cross-harmonic and cross-component expectation terms** when powers are summed (and removes any
deterministic-carrier mean component). That is the correct argument; the $|EX|^2$-versus-variance
discussion in §2.2 is the same fact and should be cross-referenced rather than restated.

**Correct every occurrence** of the false reason to the cross-term/zero-mean reason. Do not weaken
the requirement itself: uniform initial residual phases, independent per harmonic, shared across
microphones, remain part of the render contract. Grep for `increment kernel`, `non-stationary
mean`, and `no longer` to find them all.
