## Verdict

Yes: each legacy arm reads its own family's DREGON-like bank audio at least as
well as it reads real DREGON cruise — median 2.86 rev/s (`rig_easy`, against
4.88 real) and 3.61 (`rig_hard`, against 3.18) — and the converse does not
hold, because the v2 arms score 17.49 and 28.28 on that same legacy DREGON
audio, two to three times their own real DREGON cruise numbers (8.85, 10.52).
The stage-2 DREGON anchor the bank was drawn around is harder than the bank for
`rig_easy` (5.95, q1-q3 2.44-10.15) and the same as the bank for `rig_hard`
(3.57, 2.38-12.08); the v2 arms sit at 10.74 and 28.92 on it. On the v2 family's
own DREGON-entry clips the ordering does not flip back symmetrically: `nv2_easy`
has the best cell in the table (1.79) but `nv2_hard` is at 7.84, while the two
legacy arms are at 4.00 and 2.27 on those same clips — i.e. the legacy arms stay
within ~2 rev/s of the v2 arms on v2 audio, whereas the v2 arms are 6-25 rev/s
behind the legacy arms on legacy audio. The real-data-trained reference
`real_r4_scv2_unified` is worse on every synthetic condition (5.86-18.16) than
on the real DREGON cruise frames it was trained for (3.54). Nothing collapsed
the rotors onto one speed anywhere: the labels' median frame-mean peak-to-peak
spread is 4.93 rev/s on the legacy conditions and 8.33 on the v2 condition, and
every model's median predicted spread is between 3.1 and 12.8 rev/s.

Two facts limit how far these cells can be read. (1) The `v2_stream`
trajectory draws its OWN bank entry to pick the flight, so although the AUDIO of
`v2_bank_dregon` is always a DREGON v2 entry, 5 of its 6 windows flew a michaels
flight (clip 1 is the only DREGON one; `clips[].traj_rig` in `scores.json`
records each). (2) Six clips per cell is a small sample and several IQRs are
very wide (`nv2_easy` spans 2.94-41.02 on `legacy_bank_dregon`), so only the
large gaps above are separated by the spread.

Scope: this pass scored 5 scv2-family models x 3 conditions x 6 clips. The two
fitted-trajectory conditions (`legacy_bank_dregon_fittedtraj`, `v2_fit_dregon`
— DREGON-like audio flown at real-like DREGON speeds) and the four HPPNet
models are defined in `scripts/_legacy_self_check.py` and reachable with
`--conditions` / `--models`, but were not run here, so the question of how the
legacy models do on their own audio at real-like speeds is still open.
