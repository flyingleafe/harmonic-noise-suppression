A. One stochastic process and one carrier correction
For each rig, clip c, rotor r:
f0_r(t)=raw_r(t-delay_r)+b_c,r (Hz).
dnu_r=-lambda_r nu_r dt + sqrt(2lambda_r sigma_r^2)dW_r; dtheta_r=nu_r dt.
depsilon_rk=sqrt(2D_rk)dW_rk.
Phi_r(t)=2pi integral f0_r + theta_r(t).
x_m(t)=sum_rk A_mrk(t)cos(kPhi_r(t)+epsilon_rk(t)+alpha_mrk)+u_m(t).
No second unconstrained per-frame spline/carrier correction. The time-resolved inferred carrier correction is E[nu_r(t)|audio,raw]/2pi; total includes b_c,r and delay. State grid chosen by dynamics bandwidth, independent of long spectral window; posterior correction must resolve subframe motion. Exact coupled integrated-OU discrete transition/covariance from supplied docs, numerically stable small-step expansions. b is perclip random effect with calibrated prior and a population/recording mean; constrain mean OU to stationaryzero in law, anchor theta(t0)=0 and source phase gauge. Finite-record bias/slowOU confounding must be reported, not fixed by two free paths.
Independent acoustic epsilon never enters physical shaft RPS. Renderer must emit audio plus realized interval-average shaftRPS from deltaPhi/(2pi dt). One theta shared across harmonics AND microphones; epsilon source-shared across microphones. No tonebank/noise split for revised model.

Main baseline inference is damped sparse MAP / iterative linearization for latent phases with conditional amplitude solves; approximate Laplace/generalizedEM dynamic updates, not exactO(K) Kalman. Complex observations need actual integration of latent path inside window or equivalent sufficiently resolved heterodyned subbands; cannot factor residual exp(i theta_at_center) outside a long moving-frame atom unless approximation measured acceptable.
Prior prediction and conditional reconstruction are DIFFERENT:
E|X(f)|^2=sum_ts w_t w_s A_t A_s exp(i(phi0_t-phi0_s+m_t-m_s-2pi f(t-s))) exp(-0.5*(Ctt+Css-2Cts)).
Heldout prediction uses m=0,C=prior. Conditional reconstruction uses posterior m,Cpost. NEVER center on posterior path and multiply full prior OU linewidth. Primary heldout predictive synth uses only raw heldout telemetry + training-derived bias law + one freshOU draw; no refined labels or inferred heldout-audio corrections. Posterior replay diagnostics allowed separately.
