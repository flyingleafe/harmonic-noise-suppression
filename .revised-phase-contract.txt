SCIENTIFIC REVIEW DECISIONS / ITEMS NOT YET SOLVED:
- Overlap need not be banned: marginal Whittle terms still define a valid composite/quasi score, but neither independent posterior curvature nor raw term-count certainty is valid. Densify latent-state grid independently of spectral window width; overlap alone does not resolve subwindow dynamics. A dense Gaussian likelihood for redundant complete STFT arrays is singular unless handled on its true support. Main must choose/freeze dependence-aware fitting/score after review, not silently rename ordinary summed Whittle exact joint likelihood.
- Moving ramps require actual within-window integrated carrier atoms, not mere frame-mean speed or broaden-by-slope heuristic.
- Shared k-scaled integrated OU plus independent harmonic Wiener phases is a candidate physical mechanism, not established fact. Preserve complex tracks/cross-order increment evidence; independent uniform initial harmonic phases mean ordinary cross-harmonic complex covariance need not expose this dependence.
- Carrier gauge: don't optimize unrestricted per-frame carrier correction AND unrestricted low-frequency shaft path independently. Per-clip bias plus one shared time-resolved shaft/carrier state must be identifiable; don't infer a path then integrate its same prior variance into spectral linewidth again. Explicitly distinguish telemetry-conditioned posterior trajectory, physical input carrier and fresh renderer jitter to avoid double-counting or corrupted synthetic RPS targets.
- Sparse MAP smoothing is defensible; the docs' exact O(K) Kalman assertion is NOT established by rank-one process covariance (conditioning creates residual correlations). Likewise Gaussian prior on log D is not automatically conjugate to noisy moment-derived D. Correct these claims before implementation.
- Per-clip/per-regime free level can absorb the entire speed law; tie recording gain across regimes, anchor speed law, and don't infer general speed dynamics from cruise alone.
